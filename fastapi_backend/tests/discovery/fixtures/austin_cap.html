Customer Assistance Program (CAP)

The City of Austin Customer Assistance Program helps income-qualified Austin Energy and Austin Water customers lower their monthly utility bills.

Who is eligible

Households that receive Medicaid, SNAP, or other qualifying assistance, or whose income is at or below 200% of the federal poverty level, may qualify.

Benefits

Eligible customers receive a discount of 10% on electric charges and reduced water and wastewater fees.

How to apply

Apply online or call 512-494-9400. Apply for CAP online
